Transcend Snowflake Native App

I recently built Transcend’s Snowflake Native App end to end. The product syncs consent and preference decisions into the customer’s Snowflake environment and enforces them using native Row Access Policies and masking.

The product bet: move governance from the application edge into the data layer where the customer’s data actually lives. The hard product problems are the useful ones—permissions, schema drift, deployment, and in-warehouse reliability.
