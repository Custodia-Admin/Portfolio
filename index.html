02 / How I work

Less theatre.
More product.

My best work comes from staying close to the system, the customer workflow, and the commercial model at the same time. Strategy is useful when it survives implementation.

01

Start with the operating reality.

Understand where the work actually happens, what fails today, and which constraint is structural rather than incidental.

02

Make architecture a product decision.

APIs, permissions, deployment boundaries, data location, and failure behavior all shape the customer experience.

03

Productize the repeatable part.

Use enterprise requests to uncover patterns—not to accumulate a permanent layer of bespoke exceptions.

04

Stay close enough to build.

Prototype, test integrations, inspect data, and understand the system deeply enough to make better product tradeoffs.
